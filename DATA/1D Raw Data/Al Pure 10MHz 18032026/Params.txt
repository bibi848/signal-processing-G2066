This data is for a rotation experiment conducted with a pure sample of aluminium. 
The rig is not good, hence the data may also be utterly worthless. A future experiment will 
include a much better experimental setup. 
The experiment takes 3 images for speed of sound calculations.
Then full images are taken of the block until it spins 180 degrees. 
The block is moved 5mm along its longest axis and the experiment is repeated. 

The depth of the block is 50mm
The block is moved 5mm parallel to the first scan.
Array always spun anticlockwise with respect to the material.

Speed of sound measurements:
Imasonic 1D 64els 5.00MHz 0.63mm pitch
FMC
Sample Frequency: 25MHz
Time Points: 1200
Gain: 40dB
Averages: 64

Rotation Measurements:
Imasonic 1D 128els 10MHz 0.30mm pitch
FMC
Sample Frequency: 50MHz
Time Points: 1200
Gain: 70dB
Averages: 64
